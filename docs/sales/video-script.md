# Demo video voice-over

About 180 words, a little over a minute. Read one line, pause for about 2 seconds, then read the
next. Claude splits the recording at the pauses and times each scene of the video to its line.
The video is re-recorded to fit, so nothing is stretched (D50).

**Recording:** a phone voice memo is fine. Find a quiet room with soft furnishings, hold the
phone about a hand's width from your mouth, slightly to one side, and talk the way you'd
explain it to a friend. If you stumble, pause and read that line again; the last take of each
line is the one used.

**Accuracy:** every claim below is something the product does today. If you want to change a
word, change it here first, so the claims stay accurate.

1. *(title)* This is Breakline: a lock that sits outside your RMM. If your RMM is ever the way
   in, it shuts remote access at every client site, and someone holding your console can't
   open it. Everything you're about to see is real: our cloud, a gateway box, and a clinic PC
   sending real traffic.
2. *(sign in)* You sign in and add a passkey. Opening access always needs one.
3. *(enrol)* To put a box at a client site, you click enrol, confirm with your passkey, and
   run one command on the box. It makes its own key. There are no certificates to manage.
4. *(locked)* By default it's locked. The clinic PC can't reach AnyDesk, by name or by
   address, but the normal internet still works.
5. *(open)* Need to work on the site? Open a window: fifteen minutes, approved with a passkey.
   Now the remote tools connect, and the box already knows when the window ends, so it closes
   on time even if it crashes.
6. *(lock now)* Here a technician is connected. Hit lock now, and the live session is cut on
   the spot. That's exactly what happens at every site, automatically, when your RMM lands on
   CISA's list of exploited vulnerabilities. You reopen with a passkey once you've patched.
7. *(record)* Every open, close and approval goes on a tamper-evident record you can export
   for clients and insurers.
8. *(pilot)* If you're an Australian MSP, we're running free monitor-only pilots. Nothing is
   blocked for the first few weeks. You just see when your remote tools reach a site.
