# Outreach drafts

Drafts for the validation in `docs/STRATEGY.md`. Rules for every message:

- Claude drafts. The owner reads it, changes what they like, and sends it from their own
  email or phone. Nothing goes out without that.
- The first contact asks for a conversation, not a sale. Say plainly that we are building
  something; don't pitch it until they ask.
- Never name another client or MSP, and never claim compliance, certification or an
  insurance benefit.

## 1. Text to your dad

> Hey Dad, I'm working on a small security product for the IT companies that look after
> clinics' computers. Two quick questions. Who looks after the IT at your practice? Just the
> company name is fine. And would you be up for a 20 minute chat one evening about how they
> get into your computers to fix things? It's not a sales thing, I just want to know how it
> works in a real practice.

Why: he gives us a clinic-side interview (questions 1–14 of the clinic script in the market
scan), and his practice's IT provider is exactly the customer we want to talk to.

## 2. For your dad to forward to his IT provider

**Subject:** Quick question from [dad's name]'s son (20 minutes, no sales)

> Hi [first name],
>
> I'm [your name], [dad's name]'s son. I'm building a small security tool for MSPs, and
> before I go any further I want to understand how MSPs actually run remote access across
> their clients, especially after this year's N-central news.
>
> Would you have 20 minutes for a call in the next couple of weeks? I won't pitch
> anything. I'm happy to share what I hear from other MSPs (without names).
>
> Thanks,
> [your name]
> [phone]

## 3. Cold email to an MSP (once the target list is ready)

**Subject:** After N-central: 20 minutes on how you handle remote access?

> Hi [first name],
>
> I'm [your name], building a small security tool for Australian MSPs. After the ACSC's
> warning about N-central in August, I'm talking to MSPs about how they run remote access
> across their clients, and what they'd want if their own RMM were ever the way in.
>
> Would you have 20 minutes in the next two weeks? No pitch. I'll share what I hear from
> other MSPs, without names.
>
> [your name]
> [phone]

Personalise the first line from the research list (their sectors, their city, a case study
of theirs). Send at most 5 a week, so every reply gets a fast, personal answer.

## 4. On the call

- Use the MSP script in section 10.2 of the market scan, then the three offer questions in
  `docs/STRATEGY.md`, in that order.
- Write down what they **did** last time, any money they spent, and any commitment they make
  (an intro, a pilot, another meeting). Opinions about the idea count for little.
- After each call, send Claude the notes, rough is fine. Claude keeps the tally against the
  gate.
